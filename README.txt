TapForge Auto-Clicker
=====================

Download: https://github.com/saberapexyt-commits/TapForge/releases/latest/download/TapForge.exe

Just download TapForge.exe and double-click it. Nothing else to install.
Put it anywhere you like (Desktop, a Games folder...). TapForge updates itself:
when a new version is out it asks you, downloads it and restarts.

First launch: Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app
isn't signed by a big company. Click "More info" then "Run anyway".

Controls
- F6 starts / stops clicking, F7 is the emergency stop (change them in Settings > Keybinds)
- Mouse 4 / Mouse 5 can also be used as hotkeys
- Settings, presets and stats are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\TapForge

Features
- Left, right or middle click, single or double, or a keyboard key
- Speed by interval (ms / sec / min) or clicks per second (up to 1000 with Extended speed)
- High-resolution click engine on its own thread
- Stop after a number of clicks or a time limit
- Click points with optional random radius, process filter, screen-corner/edge safety stops
- Button hold (duty cycle), speed randomization, toggle or hold hotkey
- Light/dark theme, custom accent colors, compact mode, tray mode, presets, usage stats
